# Product Requirements Document

## Product vision

Build an enterprise AI-powered media platform that lets teams ingest, organize, enrich, search, create, and distribute media safely at scale.

## Goals

- Centralize media assets and metadata.
- Use AI to automate transcription, tagging, summarization, moderation, and discovery.
- Provide secure collaboration, role-based access, and auditability.
- Support API-first integrations and reliable production operations.

## Primary users

- Content teams managing media libraries and publishing workflows.
- Editors and producers creating, reviewing, and approving assets.
- Administrators governing access, retention, and compliance.
- Developers integrating media capabilities into internal products.

## Core capabilities

1. Asset ingestion, storage, versioning, and lifecycle management.
2. AI enrichment for video, audio, image, and document assets.
3. Semantic and metadata search with saved collections.
4. Review, approval, and publishing workflows.
5. Organizations, workspaces, users, roles, and audit logs.
6. APIs, webhooks, and analytics.

## Success measures

- Time from upload to searchable asset.
- Search-to-use conversion and asset reuse rate.
- Workflow completion time and approval turnaround.
- AI enrichment accuracy, cost per asset, and failure rate.
- Availability, latency, and security incident rate.

## Scope boundaries

The initial release prioritizes web-based asset management and AI enrichment. Native apps, advanced editing, and marketplace features remain future considerations.
